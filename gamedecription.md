## **The Four Immortals Against the Northern Monster**

### **Genre**

Action Roguelike • Hack-and-Slash • Mythological Fantasy

### **Platform**

PC / Console (Controller-supported)

---

## **Game Overview**

**The Four Immortals Against the Northern Monster** is a fast-paced **roguelike action game** rooted in Eastern mythology. Players take on the roles of four legendary Immortals, each wielding divine powers, as they descend into the frozen northern realms to defeat an ancient, world-devouring monster threatening the balance between heaven and earth.

Inspired by the **fluid combat of *Hades*** and the **precision platforming and progression systems of *Dead Cells***, the game emphasizes skill-based combat, replayability, and dynamic builds that change every run.

Each attempt into the Northern Lands is unique. Death is not failure—it is part of the journey toward mastery.

---

## **Narrative Setting**

Long ago, the **Northern Monster** was sealed beneath the frozen wastelands by the Immortals themselves. Over centuries, the seal has weakened. Corrupted by dark frost energy, the monster now commands legions of warped creatures, turning once-sacred lands into hostile domains.

The Heavenly Council summons the **Four Immortals**—each representing a different divine path—to stop the invasion. However, the northern realm twists fate itself. Memory persists through death, but the path forward constantly shifts.

The Immortals must fall, rise, and grow stronger with each attempt, uncovering fragments of forgotten lore and truths about the monster—and themselves.

---

## **Playable Characters – The Four Immortals**

Each Immortal offers a **distinct combat style**, skill tree, and build synergy:

1. **The Flame Immortal**

   * High-speed melee combat
   * Fire-based area damage and aggressive playstyle
   * Excels at close-range burst damage

2. **The Storm Immortal**

   * Agile ranged combat with lightning and wind
   * High mobility and crowd control
   * Rewards precision and positioning

3. **The Earth Immortal**

   * Heavy weapons and defensive abilities
   * Crowd suppression, shields, and counterattacks
   * Ideal for tactical, slower-paced combat

4. **The Shadow Immortal**

   * Stealth, poison, and rapid strikes
   * High-risk, high-reward gameplay
   * Focuses on mobility, debuffs, and assassination

Players unlock additional abilities, passive traits, and weapon variations through repeated runs.

## **Core Gameplay**

### **Combat System**

* Fast, responsive hack-and-slash combat
* Dodge-based defense with invincibility frames
* Skill cooldown management and combo chaining
* Enemy patterns designed to reward observation and mastery

Combat is designed to feel **tight, fluid, and punishing**, encouraging players to learn enemy behaviors and optimize builds.

---

### **Roguelike Progression**

Each run features:

* **Procedurally generated maps**
* Randomized enemy encounters
* Variable room modifiers and environmental hazards
* Dynamic boss variations

Upon death:

* Temporary upgrades are lost
* Permanent progression unlocks new abilities, weapons, and narrative content
* Story elements evolve based on player progress

No two runs play the same.

---

### **Blessings and Build Customization**

During a run, players collect **Divine Blessings**, artifacts, and cursed relics:

* Modify attacks, skills, and movement
* Enable powerful synergies between abilities
* Encourage experimentation with playstyles

Players can create:

* Glass-cannon builds
* Status-effect-focused strategies
* Defensive, sustain-heavy setups
* High-mobility assassin builds

Below is a **clear, production-ready design of the Core Gameplay Loop** for ***The Four Immortals Against the Northern Monster***, structured the way it would appear in a **GDD (Game Design Document)** or **publisher pitch**.
I include **three layers**: the **primary loop**, the **meta-progression loop**, and the **moment-to-moment combat loop**.

---

# 1. Primary Core Gameplay Loop (Roguelike Run)

This is the **main player-facing loop** repeated every run.

```
[Start Run]
     ↓
[Choose Immortal]
     ↓
[Enter Procedural Zone]
     ↓
[Combat Encounter]
     ↓
[Collect Rewards]
(Blessings / Relics / Currency)
     ↓
[Build Customization]
(Skills, Passives, Synergies)
     ↓
[Next Room or Event]
     ↓
[Mini-Boss / Boss Fight]
     ↓
[Victory?]
   ↙        ↘
[Continue]  [Death]
     ↓          ↓
[Final Boss]  [Meta Progression]
     ↓          ↓
[Ending / Unlocks]
     ↺──────────↩
```

### Design Intent

* Death is **expected**, not punitive
* Player knowledge persists
* Builds feel **temporary**, progression feels **permanent**

---

# 2. Meta-Progression Loop (Between Runs)

This loop ensures **long-term retention and mastery**.

```
[Death or Run Completion]
          ↓
[Return to Celestial Hub]
          ↓
[Spend Persistent Resources]
 - Unlock new abilities
 - Upgrade Immortal skill trees
 - Unlock weapons & blessings
          ↓
[Narrative Advancement]
 - Dialogue changes
 - Lore fragments unlocked
          ↓
[Difficulty Modifiers Unlocked]
 - Higher risk / reward
          ↓
[Start New Run]
          ↺
```

### Key Design Pillars

* Narrative unfolds **across failures**
* Player power increases **horizontally** (options), not just vertically (stats)
* Encourages experimentation, not grinding

---

# 3. Moment-to-Moment Combat Loop

This defines **feel**, heavily inspired by *Hades* and *Dead Cells*.

```
[Enemy Telegraph]
        ↓
[Player Decision]
(Dodge / Attack / Skill)
        ↓
[Action Execution]
 - Combo
 - Skill activation
 - Positioning
        ↓
[Enemy Response]
 - Counter
 - New pattern
        ↓
[Damage / Status Effects]
        ↓
[Cooldown & Resource Check]
        ↓
[Reposition]
        ↺
```

### Combat Design Goals

* Tight input response
* Clear visual telegraphing
* Skill-based survival
* High mobility, low downtime

---

# 4. Build Evolution Loop (Within a Run)

This loop drives **strategic depth**.

```
[Acquire Blessing]
        ↓
[Synergy Evaluation]
 - Does it modify attack?
 - Does it scale with current build?
        ↓
[Build Commitment]
 - Lean into damage
 - Lean into control
 - Lean into survivability
        ↓
[Difficulty Scaling]
 - Enemies adapt
 - Boss mechanics evolve
        ↓
[Build Stress Test]
(Boss Fight)
        ↺
```

### Important Principle

Every reward should **force a meaningful decision**, never a “no-brainer” upgrade.
